CHEESEBURGER


Description: A delicious cheeseburger with a juicy beef patty, melted cheese, and fresh vegetables.


Ingredients:

  • Bread
  • Beef patty
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Letuce

Steps:

  1. Preheat the pan to medium-high heat.
  2. Place the beef patty in the pan and cook for 3-4 minutes on each side, or until desired doneness is reached.
  3. While the beef is cooking, toast the bread slices.
  4. Assemble the cheeseburger by placing the beef patty on the bottom bun, followed by the melted cheese and lettuce.
  5. Top with the top bun and serve immediately.
