PIZZA BBQ


Description: A delicious pizza with a BBQ sauce base, topped with mozzarella cheese and crispy bacon.


Ingredients:

  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Bacon
  • BBQ sauce

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Transfer the dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  4. Spread the BBQ sauce over the dough.
  5. Top with mozzarella cheese and cooked bacon.
  6. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown.
